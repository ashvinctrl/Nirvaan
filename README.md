# 🌿 Nirvaan

A cutting-edge **AI-powered mental health companion** designed to offer personalized support, well-being tracking, and real-time insights.
---

## 🚀 Features

✨ **AI-Driven Conversations**  
Engage with a virtual mental health assistant that adapts to your needs.

📊 **Mood Tracking**  
Record and visualize your daily moods to monitor long-term well-being.

💡 **Personalized Recommendations**  
Receive tailored self-care tips, exercises, and activities.

🔒 **Secure & Private**  
Designed with **user privacy and data protection** in mind.

---

## 💻 Technologies & Languages

- **Frontend:** React.js  
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js  
- **AI/ML:** Python, TensorFlow (or other ML libraries)  
- **Database:** MongoDB  
- **Containerization:** Docker
